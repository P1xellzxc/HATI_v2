HATI2 spent over time

Mock of the "At a glance" chart: one line per expense type, and a dashed line for the moving average of all spending. Tap or drag on a chart for that day's numbers; tap a legend chip to hide or show a line. Example data.

Trip, day by day

Tokyo, 10 days. Lines for the top 3 types plus everything else; the dashed line is the 3-day average of all spending.

Household, month by month

Bebetime, 6 months. The dashed line is the 3-month average of all spending, so a one-off big month doesn't look like a trend.

hati-design

Lines are thin ink-weight strokes on the glass card; the average is the one heavy, dashed line, so it reads first without a colour.

dataviz rules

One axis in money. Fixed colour order by type (the colour follows the type when others are hidden). More than 3 types fold into "Other".

hati-a11y

Colours pass colour-blind checks; in light mode three lines are under 3:1, so every line has a legend chip with its total, tap values and a numbers table.

hati-money-parity

Lines and the average come from the same integer cents as the balance; the types add up to the day's total.
