Manga ink on paper. Flat paper, 2px ink borders, 3px corners, hard offset shadow only on pressable things, ruled ledger lists, numbered volume spines, screentone on the balance panel, drawn line icons, Black Han Sans + DM Sans.

- Avoid AI-slop tells: see [[hati-slop-rubric]] (manga acetate glass allowed only for floating layers).
- Every screen passes the [[hati-lazy-check]] and the [[hati-pixel-perfectionist]] measurements.
- Words follow [[hati-copy]].
- Colors: [[Design/Wada themes|Wada themes]].
